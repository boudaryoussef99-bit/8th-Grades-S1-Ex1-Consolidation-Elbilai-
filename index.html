Reading Text: Unexpected Discoveries

The Silver Pocket Watch: In 2018, a teenager named Lucas was diving in a shallow lake near Lyon, France, when he felt something hard under the mud. He pulled out a heavy silver pocket watch. The glass was cracked, but the metal case was intact. Engraved on the back was a date: 12 June 1944, along with the initials "P.M.". After local newspapers shared the story, the grandson of a World War II soldier contacted Lucas. The watch belonged to Pierre Martin, who lost it during a river crossing 74 years earlier.

The Viking Ring: In 2021, while walking his dog along a muddy path in York, England, a man named Arthur discovered a small gold ring shining in the grass. It was decorated with intricate Viking patterns. Arthur took it to a local museum, where historians confirmed it was over 1,000 years old. The museum director, Dr. Helen Vance, stated that such rings were extremely rare in the region. Arthur was thrilled to be part of such an incredible historical find, and the ring is now displayed in the city's museum.
