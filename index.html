Reading Text: Practice Passages

The Secret in the Sand: In 2023, a teenager named Ethan found a plastic bottle on a beach in Miami, USA. Inside was a piece of paper with a letter dated 15 July 2010. The letter was written by a boy named Noah, aged 13, from Boston, Massachusetts. The letter asked if anyone would ever find it and requested the finder to contact his school address. Noah was 26 years old when Ethan was able to contact him through social media. Ethan found the bottle only a few miles away from where Noah had dropped it into the ocean years earlier.

A Message Across the Sea: In 2021, Sophia Martinez was walking along a rocky shore in Sydney, Australia, when she spotted a waterproof pouch. Inside was a postcard written in Spanish from an eight-year-old girl named Sofia from Madrid, Spain, thousands of kilometres away. She was looking for a penpal to practice writing. After the local radio broadcasted the story, the girl's family reached out to Sophia. Sofia (the writer) was now eleven years old and living in Valencia, Spain.
